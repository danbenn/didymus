### Seeking Peace Biblical Teaching On Peace

In	  our	  daily	  life,	  we	  often	  do	  not	  have	  peace	  but	  rather	  conflict. We	  also	  have	  a	  hard	  time	  knowing	  whom	  to	  trust:

Ps.	  28:3: Do	  not	  drag	  me	  off	  with	  the	  wicked, with	  the	  workers	  of	  evil, who	  speak	  peace	  with	  their	  neighbors while	  evil	  is	  in	  their	  hearts.

Ps.	  35:20: For	  they	  do	  not	  speak	  peace, but	  against	  those	  who	  are	  quiet	  in	  the	  land they	  devise	  words	  of	  deceit.

Ps.	  120:6-­‐7: Too	  long	  have	  I	  had	  my	  dwelling	  among	  those	  who	  hate	  peace. I	  am	  for	  peace, but	  when	  I	  speak,	  they	  are	  for	  war!

Is.	  59:8: The	  way	  of	  peace	  they	  do	  not	  know, and	  there	  is	  no	  justice	  in	  their	  paths; they	  have	  made	  their	  roads	  crooked; no	  one	  who	  treads	  on	  them	  knows	  peace.

Jer.	  9:8: Their	  tongue	  is	  a	  deadly	  arrow; it	  speaks	  deceitfully; with	  his	  mouth	  each	  speaks	  peace	  to	  his	  neighbor, but	  in	  his	  heart	  he	  plans	  an	  ambush	  for	  him.

Gen.	  37:4: But	  when	  his	  brothers	  saw	  that	  their	  father	  loved	  him	  more	  than	  all	  his	  brothers, they	  hated	  him	  and	  could	  not	  speak	  peacefully	  to	  him.

As	  a	  result,	  we	  often	  think	  and	  act	  out	  of	  fear. The	  opposite	  of	  peace	  is	  fear.

Gen.	  43:23a: He	  replied,	  “Peace	  to	  you,	  do	  not	  be	  afraid.”

Lev.	  26:6: I	  will	  give	  peace	  in	  the	  land,	  and	  you	  shall	  lie	  down,	  and	  none	  shall	  make	  you	  afraid. And	  I	  will	  remove	  harmful	  beasts	  from	  the	  land,	  and	  the	  sword	  shall	  not	  go	  through your	  land.

Only	  God	  can	  make	  real	  peace,	  so	  that	  trust	  can	  be	  restored:

Num.	  6:26: the	  Lord	  lift	  up	  his	  face	  upon	  you	  and	  give	  you	  peace.

Judges	  6:22-­‐24: But	  the	  LORD	  said	  to	  him,	  “Peace	  be	  to	  you.	  Do	  not	  fear;	  you	  shall	  not	  die.”	  	  Then Gideon	  built	  an	  altar	  there	  to	  the	  LORD	  and	  called	  it,	  The	  LORD	  Is	  Peace.

Judges	  18:6: And	  the	  priest	  said	  to	  them,	  “Go	  in	  peace.	  The	  journey	  on	  which	  you	  go	  is	  under	  the eye	  of	  the	  Lord.”

Job	  25:2 “Dominion	  and	  fear	  are	  with	  God; he	  makes	  peace	  in	  his	  high	  heaven.”

Ps.	  4:8: In	  peace	  I	  will	  both	  lie	  down	  and	  sleep; for	  you	  alone,	  O	  LORD,	  make	  me	  dwell	  in	  safety.

Ps.	  29:11: May	  the	  Lord	  give	  strength	  to	  his	  people! May	  the	  Lord	  bless	  his	  people	  with	  peace!

Ps.	  85:8: Let	  me	  hear	  what	  God	  the	  Lord	  will	  speak,	  for	  he	  will	  speak	  peace	  to	  his	  people,	  to his	  saints;	  but	  let	  them	  not	  turn	  back	  to	  foolishness.

Ps.	  54:10: For	  the	  mountains	  may	  depart and	  the	  hills	  be	  removed, but	  my	  steadfast	  love	  shall	  not	  depart	  from	  you, and	  my	  covenant	  of	  peace	  shall	  not	  be	  removed,” says	  the	  Lord,	  who	  has	  compassion	  on	  you.

God’s	  peace	  comes	  to	  us	  through	  Jesus	  Christ:

Zech.	  9:9-­‐10: Behold,	  your	  king	  is	  coming	  to	  you… and	  he	  shall	  speak	  peace	  to	  the	  nations; his	  rule	  shall	  be	  from	  sea	  to	  sea, and	  from	  the	  River	  to	  the	  ends	  of	  the	  earth

Is.	  9:6-­‐7a: For	  to	  us	  a	  child	  is	  born,

to	  us	  a	  son	  is	  given; and	  the	  government	  shall	  be	  upon	  his	  shoulder, and	  his	  name	  shall	  be	  called Wonderful	  Counselor,	  Mighty	  God, Everlasting	  Father,	  Prince	  of	  Peace. Of	  the	  increase	  of	  his	  government	  and	  of	  peace there	  will	  be	  no	  end,

Is.	  52:7	  (compare	  Nahum	  1:15): How	  beautiful	  upon	  the	  mountains are	  the	  feet	  of	  him	  who	  brings	  good	  news, who	  publishes	  peace,	  who	  brings	  good	  news	  of	  happiness, who	  publishes	  salvation, who	  says	  to	  Zion,	  “Your	  God	  reigns.”

Is.	  53:5: But	  he	  was	  pierced	  for	  our	  transgressions; he	  was	  crushed	  for	  our	  iniquities; upon	  him	  was	  the	  chastisement	  that	  brought	  us	  peace, and	  with	  his	  wounds	  we	  are	  healed.

Eph.	  2:13-­‐15: But	  now	  in	  Christ	  Jesus	  you	  who	  once	  were	  far	  off	  have	  been	  brought	  near	  by	  the blood	  of	  Christ.	  For	  he	  himself	  is	  our	  peace,	  who	  has	  made	  us	  both	  one	  and	  has broken	  down	  in	  his	  flesh	  the	  dividing	  wall	  of	  hostility	  by	  abolishing	  the	  law	  of commandments	  expressed	  in	  ordinances,	  that	  he	  might	  create	  in	  himself	  one	  new man	  in	  place	  of	  the	  two,	  so	  making	  peace,

Jesus	  alone	  has	  the	  power	  to	  move	  us	  out	  of	  our	  fear	  into	  peace. This	  is	  what	  he	  longs	  to	  do	  for	  us.

Jn.	  14:27: Peace	  I	  leave	  with	  you;	  my	  peace	  I	  give	  to	  you.	  Not	  as	  the	  world	  gives	  do	  I	  give	  to you.	  Let	  not	  your	  hearts	  be	  troubled,	  neither	  let	  them	  be	  afraid.

Jn.	  20:19: On	  the	  evening	  of	  that	  day,	  the	  first	  day	  of	  the	  week,	  the	  doors	  being	  locked	  where the	  disciples	  were	  for	  fear	  of	  the	  Jews,	  Jesus	  came	  and	  stood	  among	  them	  and	  said	  to them,	  “Peace	  be	  with	  you.”

For	  two	  people	  to	  be	  at	  peace,	  the	  Lord	  must	  be	  between	  them	  and	  be	  their bond	  of	  peace:

1	  Sam.	  20:42: Then	  Jonathan	  said	  to	  David,	  “Go	  in	  peace,	  because	  we	  have	  sworn	  both	  of	  us	  in	  the name	  of	  the	  Lord,	  saying,	  ‘The	  Lord	  shall	  be	  between	  me	  and	  you,	  and	  between	  my offspring	  and	  your	  offspring,	  forever.’”

Seeking	  peace	  is	  the	  goal	  of	  our	  lives

Ps.	  34:14	  (1	  Pet.	  3:11): Turn	  away	  from	  evil	  and	  do	  good; seek	  peace	  and	  pursue	  it.

Zech.	  8:12: For	  there	  shall	  be	  a	  sowing	  of	  peace.

Ps.	  37:37: Mark	  the	  blameless	  and	  behold	  the	  upright, for	  there	  is	  a	  future	  for	  the	  man	  of	  peace.

Ps.	  85:10: Steadfast	  love	  and	  faithfulness	  meet; righteousness	  and	  peace	  kiss	  each	  other.

Mt.	  5:9: Blessed	  are	  the	  peacemakers,	  for	  they	  shall	  be	  called	  sons	  of	  God.

Rom.:	  14:17,19: For	  the	  kingdom	  of	  God	  is	  not	  a	  matter	  of	  eating	  and	  drinking	  but	  of	  righteousness and	  peace	  and	  joy	  in	  the	  Holy	  Spirit…. So	  then	  let	  us	  pursue	  what	  makes	  for	  peace	  and	  for	  mutual	  upbuilding.

James	  3:18: And	  a	  harvest	  of	  righteousness	  is	  sown	  in	  peace	  by	  those	  who	  make	  peace.

The	  goal	  of	  peace	  cannot	  be	  achieved	  by	  forcing	  things:

Ps.	  37:11: But	  the	  meek	  shall	  inherit	  the	  land and	  delight	  themselves	  in	  abundant	  peace.

James	  3:17: But	  the	  wisdom	  from	  above	  is	  first	  pure,	  then	  peaceable,	  gentle,	  open	  to	  reason,	  full of	  mercy	  and	  good	  fruits,	  impartial	  and	  sincere.

To	  work	  for	  peace	  takes	  time	  and	  it	  often	  hurts	  or	  comes	  at	  a	  personal	  cost:

Ps.	  39:2: I	  was	  mute	  and	  silent; I	  held	  my	  peace	  to	  no	  avail,	  and	  my	  distress	  grew	  worse.

Easy,	  false	  peace	  or	  “peace”	  without	  truth	  is	  worthless	  and	  a	  trap. We	  should	  never	  settle	  for	  this,	  since	  God	  hates	  everything	  that	  is	  false	  and misleading:

Jer.	  6:14;/8:11;	  8:15/14:19: They	  have	  healed	  the	  wound	  of	  my	  people	  lightly, saying,	  ‘Peace,	  peace,’ when	  there	  is	  no	  peace…

We	  looked	  for	  peace,	  but	  no	  good	  came; for	  a	  time	  of	  healing,	  but	  behold,	  terror.

Ezek.	  13:10-­‐11a: Precisely	  because	  they	  have	  misled	  my	  people,	  saying,	  ‘Peace,’	  when	  there	  is	  no peace,	  and	  because,	  when	  the	  people	  build	  a	  wall,	  these	  prophets	  smear	  it	  with whitewash,	  say	  to	  those	  who	  smear	  it	  with	  whitewash	  that	  it	  shall	  fall!

Ps.	  69:22: Let	  their	  own	  table	  before	  them	  become	  a	  snare; and	  when	  they	  are	  at	  peace,	  let	  it	  become	  a	  trap.

James	  2:15-­‐16: If	  a	  brother	  or	  sister	  is	  poorly	  clothed	  and	  lacking	  in	  daily	  food,	  and	  one	  of	  you	  says to	  them,	  “Go	  in	  peace,	  be	  warmed	  and	  filled,”	  without	  giving	  them	  the	  things	  needed for	  the	  body,	  what	  good	  is	  that?

Real	  peace	  is	  patient	  and	  looks	  with	  the	  eyes	  of	  God	  to	  the	  well-­‐being	  of	  the whole	  person

1	  Sam.	  25:6: And	  thus	  you	  shall	  greet	  him:	  “Peace	  be	  to	  you,	  and	  peace	  be	  to	  your	  house,	  and peace	  be	  to	  all	  that	  you	  have.”

2	  Cor.	  13:11: Finally,	  brothers,	  rejoice.	  Aim	  for	  restoration,	  comfort	  one	  another,	  agree	  with	  one another,	  live	  in	  peace;	  and	  the	  God	  of	  love	  and	  peace	  will	  be	  with	  you.
