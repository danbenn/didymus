### Final Exam Prayer And Spirituality 2018

Final Exam—Prayer and Spirituality in the Early Church (THE710; Spring 2018) Supply definitions for the following twelve technical terms, which are of fundamental importance to the study of early Christian spirituality.

The Organization of Monastic Life (1) cenobium (2) lavra

Repentance (3) penthos (4) compunction

Spiritual Warfare (5) antirrhesis (6) akedia

Spiritual Guidance (7) exagoreusis logismôn ("manifestation of thoughts") (8) discretio (in Cassian)

Prayer (9) monologistic prayer

Communion with God (10) hesychia (11) apatheia (12) epektasis
