### Heart Chart How We Respond To Temptation

I live in my old nature:  I am distant from God  I try to do as much as I can in my own strength/apart from God

I ignore my inner turmoil (I Thessalonians 5:19-21) I quench the Spirit; I stay busy with “good” things I stop, am still & listen (Psalm 46:10 & 1Peter 5:6) I humble myself under God’s hand My root sins are exposed: Pride/Vainglory, Envy, Akedia, Anger, Greed, Gluttony, Lust MY CHOICE I ignore what God has revealed (Matthew 19:16-26) What “riches” am I holding onto? I TURN TO THE CROSS Psalm 121 Numbers 21:4-8 John 3:14-15

I am transformed into my new nature  I am increasingly conformed to the image of Christ  I yield the fruit of the Spirit EXTERNAL EVENT OR CIRCUMSTANCE My “Gut” Reaction (Erethismos-inward irritation) or what makes me “red hot” NO CHANGE IN MY HEART I AM TRANSFORMED BY GOD MY VISIBLE ACTIONS I lift my eyes to the God who heals me; I yield to Him
