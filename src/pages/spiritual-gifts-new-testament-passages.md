### Spiritual Gifts: New Testament Passages

**Rom. 1:11** (ESV): "For I long to see you, that I may impart to you some spiritual **gift** to strengthen you—"

**Rom. 5:15-16** (ESV): "But the **free gift** is not like the trespass. For if many died through one man's trespass, much more have the grace of God and the free gift by the grace of that one man Jesus Christ abounded for many. And the free gift (*dōrēma*) is not like the result of that one man's sin. For the judgment following one trespass brought condemnation, but the free gift following many trespasses brought justification."

**Rom. 6:23** (ESV): "For the wages of sin is death, but the **free gift** of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord."

**Rom. 11:29** (ESV): "For the **gifts** and the calling of God are irrevocable."

**Rom. 12:6** (ESV): "Having **gifts** that differ according to the grace given to us, let us use them: if prophecy, in proportion to our faith;"

**1 Cor. 1:7** (ESV): "so that you are not lacking in any **gift**, as you wait for the revealing of our Lord Jesus Christ,"

**1 Cor. 7:7** (ESV): "I wish that all were as I myself am. But each has his own **gift** from God, one of one kind and one of another."

**1 Cor. 12:4** (ESV): "Now there are varieties of **gifts**, but the same Spirit;"

- Compare 1 Cor. 12:6: "and there are varieties of activities (*energemata*), but it is the same God who empowers them all in everyone."

Here the *energemata* (activities that arise from God acting) are identical with the *charismata* (gifts given by God through the Spirit). So Pseudo-Zonaras Lexicogr., *Lexicon* (A.D. 13th c.), alphabetic letter epsilon, page 740 line 6: Ἐνεργημάτων. ἀντὶ τοῦ χαρισμάτων. καὶ ὁ Ἀπόστολος· καὶ διαιρέσεις ἐνεργημάτων εἰσί. τὸ διαιρέσεις οἷον μερίδες.

**1 Cor. 12:9** (ESV): "to another faith by the same Spirit, to another **gifts** of healing by the one Spirit,"

**1 Cor. 12:28** (ESV): "And God has appointed in the church first apostles, second prophets, third teachers, then miracles, then **gifts** of healing, helping, administrating, and various kinds of tongues."

**1 Cor. 12:30-31** (ESV): "Do all possess **gifts** of healing? Do all speak with tongues? Do all interpret? But earnestly desire the higher **gifts**. And I will show you a still more excellent way."

**2 Cor. 1:11** (ESV): "You also must help us by prayer, so that many will give thanks on our behalf for the blessing (**gift**) granted us through the prayers of many."

- Note the use of the related verb in 2 Cor. 2:10 (ESV): "Anyone whom you forgive, I also forgive. Indeed, what I have forgiven (*kecharismai*), if I have forgiven (*kecharismai*) anything, has been for your sake in the presence of Christ," (*kecharismai* = freely granted pardon/forgiveness)

**1 Tim. 4:14** (ESV): "Do not neglect the **gift** you have, which was given you by prophecy when the council of elders laid their hands on you."

**2 Tim. 1:6** (ESV): "For this reason I remind you to fan into flame the **gift** of God, which is in you through the laying on of my hands,"

**1 Pet. 4:10** (ESV): "As each has received a **gift**, use it to serve one another, as good stewards of God's varied grace:"
